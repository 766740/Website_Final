Support your strength

Creatine is a naturally occurring compound that helps your muscles produce energy during intense physical activity. It is one of the most widely studied sports supplements and is commonly used by people who do strength or high-intensity training

Creatine information

What is creatine

Creatine is found naturally in foods such as meat and fish and is also produced by the body. Taking creatine as a supplement can increase the amount of creatine stored in your muscles.

Why it matters

Creatine can help support strength and performance during short, intense activities. Its effects are most useful when combined with regular training and a balanced diet

When to use it

Creatine is usually taken consistently rather than only before training. Many people take it at the same time each day, including on rest days

How much is enough

A common daily amount is around 3–5 g. A loading phase is not necessary for everyone. It is important to follow the recommended serving size and avoid taking more than you need

Responsible use of creatine

Use it responsibly

Creatine is considered safe for most healthy people when used as directed. It is not a replacement for proper training, food or rest, and it does not produce results on its own. Results can vary from person to person, so it is better to have realistic expectations

If you have any health conditions or take medication, consider talking to a doctor or nutritionist before using creatine
