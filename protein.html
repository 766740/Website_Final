More than just a supplement

Protein is an important nutrient that helps your body maintain and repair muscle. It can also help you feel full for longer. Protein supplements are mainly useful when it is difficult to get enough protein from regular food.

Protein information

What is protein

Protein is made up of amino acids and is important for muscles, skin, hair and other parts of the body. It can be found in many foods, but supplements can be an additional source of protein.

Why it matters

Protein supports muscle growth and repair and helps the body recover after physical activity. It is also an important part of a balanced diet.

When to use it

Protein supplements can be used after training, between meals or at other times when you need an easy source of protein. They are not necessary if you already get enough protein from your diet.

How much is enough

The amount of protein you need depends on your diet, body weight, activity level and goals. For most people, a varied diet provides enough protein. Taking more than you need does not automatically lead to better results.

Responsible use of protein

Use it responsibly

Protein supplements can be useful when it is difficult to get enough protein from regular food. However, they should not replace balanced meals or a healthy diet. Always check the ingredients, follow the product instructions and choose products from trusted brands.

If you have any health conditions or take medication, consider talking to a doctor or nutritionist before using protein
