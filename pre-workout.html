More energy for your workout

Pre-workout supplements are designed to help you feel more focused and energized before training. They often contain ingredients such as caffeine, beta-alanine and other compounds that may support performance during exercise.

Pre-workout information

What is pre-workout

Pre-workout supplements usually contain a combination of ingredients intended to support energy, focus and endurance during training. The exact ingredients can vary between products

Why it matters

Some pre-workout ingredients can help you feel more alert and may improve performance during intense exercise. The effects depend on the ingredients, the amount used and how your body reacts to them.

When to use it

Pre-workout is usually taken around 20–45 minutes before training. It is not necessary for everyone and does not need to be used before every workout

How much is enough

The right amount depends on the product and your tolerance, especially when it contains caffeine. Start with the recommended serving size and avoid taking more just because you want a stronger effect.

Responsible use of pre-workout

Use it responsibly

Pre-workout can be useful for some people, but it is not necessary for everyone. Products containing caffeine may cause increased heart rate, sleep problems or stomach discomfort, especially if you are sensitive to caffeine

Always follow the recommended serving size and avoid combining pre-workout with other stimulants. If you have any health conditions or take medication, consider talking to a doctor or nutritionist before using it
